About Jeanne

Jeanne has spent more than 20 years as a global executive presence and presentation trainer and coach. She works with clients from multinational corporations to tech conferences, local banks to design agencies.

She’s passionate about helping her clients create and deliver clear, focused and engaging presentations, the ones that audiences listen to, believe in and act on.

Frequently asked questions

Who does Jeanne work with?

Leaders and technical professionals, from multinational corporations to startups.

What kinds of workshops are available?

Executive, sales, town hall, conference and startup pitch workshops, or a custom workshop built around your organization’s needs. Participants create their content, practice their delivery, and give and receive feedback in the room.

Can sessions be held remotely?

Yes. Coaching is F2F in Prague or remote globally, and workshops are F2F or remote globally.

How long is a workshop?

The standard workshop is two days, but it can be adapted to your needs.

How many people can take part?

Six people working individually, or up to 36 working in groups.
